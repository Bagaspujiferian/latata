Persamaan Matematika & Invarian Finansial (Zero-Discrepancy Math)Setiap transaksi di dalam sistem wajib mematuhi persamaan akuntansi berikut tanpa selisih pembulatan:Rumus Pembagian Escrow per Toko (orders):$$\text{Gross Amount} = \sum (\text{Unit Price} \times \text{Quantity})$$$$\text{Platform Commission} = \text{ FLOOR }(\text{Gross Amount} \times \frac{\text{Fee Percent}}{100})$$$$\text{Seller Escrow Amount} = \text{Gross Amount} - \text{Platform Commission}$$Rumus Tagihan Induk Pembeli (payment_invoices):$$\text{Subtotal Amount} = \sum \text{Orders Gross Amount}$$$$\text{Payable After Wallet} = \text{Subtotal Amount} - \text{Wallet Used Amount}$$$$\text{Grand Total Midtrans} = \text{Payable After Wallet} + \text{Gateway Fee}$$Invarian Buku Besar (wallet_mutations):Untuk setiap baris mutasi dompet, sistem wajib memvalidasi sebelum INSERT:Jika direction == 'CREDIT', maka $\text{balance\_after} = \text{balance\_before} + \text{amount}$.Jika direction == 'DEBIT', maka $\text{balance\_before} \ge \text{amount}$ dan $\text{balance\_after} = \text{balance\_before} - \text{amount}$.Saldo pending_balance dan available_balance pada tabel wallets tidak boleh pernah bernilai negatif dalam kondisi apa pun.
